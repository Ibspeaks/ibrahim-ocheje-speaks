About Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje

Professional Experience, Public Service & Community Focus

A profile of Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje, his educational background, professional career, community involvement and public-service journey.

Candidate Profile

Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje

Petroleum Engineer, business executive and public-service aspirant from Ojiala Village in Ayah Ward, Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State.

Published profiles describe Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje as a petroleum engineering professional whose career has been closely associated with Nigeria's oil and gas industry.

His professional experience includes a long career with Chevron Nigeria Limited, following earlier experience in the oil-field services sector. Published reports also describe subsequent executive responsibilities in the private sector.

His public profile has included community development activities, professional engagement and participation in Nigerian politics, particularly in relation to Idah Federal Constituency and Kogi East.

Origin Ojiala, Ayah Ward, Ibaji LGA
Profession Petroleum Engineer
Education University of Ibadan
Constituency Idah Federal Constituency
Biography

Background & Professional Journey

Key areas of Engr. Ocheje's publicly reported background and career.

01

Early Life & Background

Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje is publicly reported as being from Ojiala Village, Ayah Ward, in Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State. His background has been described in published profiles as one rooted in the local community.

02

Education

Published biographies identify him as a graduate of Petroleum Engineering from the University of Ibadan. Some profiles also report that he distinguished himself academically while at the university.

03

Oil & Gas Career

Ocheje built a substantial professional career in Nigeria's oil and gas industry. Published reports state that he spent more than two decades with Chevron Nigeria Limited, including significant management experience.

04

Private Sector Experience

Following his career in the oil and gas industry, published profiles describe him as having taken on executive responsibilities in the private sector, including work connected with oil and gas asset development and management.

05

Professional Associations

Published profiles identify Engr. Ocheje as having professional links with the Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE) and the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE).

06

Community Engagement

Reports about his community activities have referenced support for local infrastructure, education and vulnerable members of the community. These claims should be independently documented where specific project details are published on this website.

Professional Experience

A Career Built Around Engineering & Energy

His professional record has largely been associated with engineering, oil and gas operations and management.

Chevron Nigeria Limited

Published reports describe a career spanning more than 20 years at Chevron Nigeria Limited, with substantial experience in management and petroleum engineering.

Oil & Gas Management

Available professional profiles also describe experience in project management, well-related operations, asset development and oil and gas management.

Executive Leadership

Published accounts state that after his Chevron career, Engr. Ocheje took on executive responsibilities in the private sector, including at Oceangate Oil & Gas.

Business Interests

Publicly available profiles have also associated him with private-sector business activities involving property, aluminium and other commercial interests.

Public & Political Journey

Participation in Public Affairs

Published reports document several stages of Engr. Ocheje's participation in Nigerian political affairs.

2014

Published reports state that Engr. Ocheje contested for the Idah Federal Constituency ticket within the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP).

2018

Reports documented his participation in the Kogi East senatorial political process and his continued involvement in party affairs.

2022

Blueprint reported that Engr. Ocheje won the PDP primary for the Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela/Odolu and Ofu Federal Constituency ahead of the 2023 general elections.

2027

His current public profile is associated with an aspiration for representation of Idah Federal Constituency in the 2027 election cycle.
