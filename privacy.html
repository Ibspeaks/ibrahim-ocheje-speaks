This Privacy Policy applies to information that may be submitted through the website of Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje and to information generated through normal use of the website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Respect for privacy is important when people interact with this website. This policy provides general information about the types of information that may be received, how such information may be used and the measures that may be taken to protect it.

By using this website or voluntarily submitting information through an available form, visitors should review this policy and understand how their information may be handled.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on the features available on the website, information voluntarily submitted by a visitor may include:

  • Name or other basic identification information.
  • Email address or telephone number where provided.
  • Messages, enquiries, feedback or other information submitted through forms.
  • Information provided when expressing interest in volunteering or participation.

Technical information may also be processed by the website hosting provider or other services used to operate the website. Such information can include browser type, device information, approximate usage information and technical logs.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through the website may be used for legitimate website and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and messages.
  • Managing volunteer or participation requests.
  • Providing information requested by visitors.
  • Improving website content and functionality.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
Important: Information should only be requested or used for purposes that are relevant to the particular website interaction.

4. Information Sharing

Personal information submitted through this website should not be sold or publicly disclosed simply because it has been submitted.

Where necessary for operating the website, information may be handled by technical service providers such as hosting, communication or form-processing providers. Such providers should only receive information necessary for the service they provide.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, court order or a legitimate governmental or regulatory requirement.

5. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites, including public institutions, electoral information services, social platforms and other third-party resources.

External websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Visitors should review the privacy practices of those websites before submitting personal information to them.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies depending on the hosting provider, website functions and services connected to the website.

Cookies may help websites remember settings, maintain functionality or understand general website usage. Where cookies or similar technologies are used, visitors should be provided with appropriate information and choices where required.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should avoid submitting highly sensitive information through ordinary website forms unless specifically required and appropriately protected.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Visitors should not submit another person's personal information without appropriate permission or lawful authority.

9. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, information practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page, together with the date on which the policy was last updated.

10. Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or about information submitted through this website, please use the website's contact page.
