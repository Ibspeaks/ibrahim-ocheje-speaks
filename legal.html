Our Approach

Principles Behind Our Website Policies

The website aims to provide useful public information while encouraging accuracy, responsible communication and respect for visitors and members of the public.

01

Transparency

Website information should be presented clearly, with appropriate distinctions between public information, opinions, proposals and confirmed information.

02

Responsible Communication

Public communication should promote constructive dialogue, respect and informed participation.

03

Privacy

Personal information submitted through website channels should be handled responsibly and in accordance with the applicable privacy principles described on this website.

04

Respect

Visitors are expected to communicate respectfully and avoid harassment, impersonation, abusive conduct and the deliberate spread of false information.

05

Accessibility

The website seeks to make its information understandable and usable across different devices and for as many visitors as reasonably possible.
