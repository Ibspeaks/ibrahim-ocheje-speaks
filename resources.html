Civic Information

Responsible Public Participation

Citizens are encouraged to rely on official information and follow applicable electoral rules.

Voter Information

For official voter information, registration services and updates, use INEC's official platforms rather than relying on unverified social-media information.

  • Confirm your voter information through official channels.
  • Keep your Permanent Voter's Card safe.
  • Follow official election instructions.
  • Check polling-unit information before election day.

Peaceful Participation

Democratic participation should be peaceful, lawful and respectful of the rights of other citizens.

  • Respect electoral laws and regulations.
  • Avoid violence, intimidation and disorder.
  • Verify claims before sharing them publicly.
  • Use appropriate channels for electoral complaints.

Official Electoral Information

INEC is the official electoral management body responsible for organising and supervising elections in Nigeria. For current election dates, voter services, guidelines and official notices, always check the Commission's website.
