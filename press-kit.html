Responsible Reporting

Guidance for Media Use

Responsible reporting helps the public distinguish verified information from rumours, assumptions and unsupported claims.

Verify Before Publication

Confirm important names, dates, quotations, electoral information and claims before publishing or broadcasting them.

Use Accurate Attribution

When information originates from a news report, interview, statement or third-party publication, identify the source appropriately.

Respect Context

Avoid presenting an isolated statement, photograph or report in a way that changes its original meaning.

Confirm Official Matters

Electoral dates, legal developments and official government announcements should be checked with the relevant authorities.
