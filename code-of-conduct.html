Responsible Participation

Code of Conduct

Principles for respectful communication, responsible participation, peaceful engagement and constructive public dialogue.

Our Standard

Respect, Responsibility and Constructive Engagement

This Code of Conduct provides general standards for people who interact with this website, participate in public engagements or contribute to discussions associated with this platform.

The aim is to encourage peaceful, respectful and issues-based participation while allowing different views to be expressed.

Our guiding principle

Public engagement should be conducted with respect for people, institutions, communities, differing opinions and the rule of law.

Code of Conduct

Standards for Public Engagement

Everyone engaging with the platform is encouraged to uphold the following principles.

01

Respect Others

Treat other people with dignity and respect, regardless of their political opinions, background, community or preferred candidate.

02

No Harassment

Do not use this platform to threaten, intimidate, harass or target individuals or groups.

03

Use Accurate Information

Avoid deliberately spreading false information, fabricated quotations, misleading claims or unverified allegations.

04

Issues-Based Discussion

Discussions should focus on policies, development priorities, representation and matters relevant to the public.

05

Peaceful Participation

Public engagement should remain peaceful. Violence, threats, incitement or encouragement of unlawful conduct are not acceptable.

06

Protect Personal Information

Do not publish another person's private information, telephone number, address or other personal details without appropriate permission.

07

No Impersonation

Do not falsely represent yourself as John Ibrahim Ocheje, an authorised representative or an official member of the platform.

08

Respect the Law

Participation and public communication should comply with applicable Nigerian laws and relevant electoral rules.

Responsible Engagement

Let's Keep Public Dialogue Constructive

Political and community discussions can involve strong disagreements. Differences of opinion should be handled through respectful conversation and lawful democratic participation.

  • Verify important information before sharing it.
  • Challenge ideas without attacking individuals.
  • Avoid inflammatory language and personal threats.
  • Respect peaceful political participation.
  • Direct serious concerns through appropriate channels.
  • Respect the privacy and dignity of other people.

Important Notice

This page sets out general standards for responsible participation on and around this public information platform. It does not replace applicable Nigerian law, electoral regulations or official guidance from competent authorities.
