Press Enquiries

A dedicated channel for journalists, media organisations and other legitimate media enquiries relating to John Ibrahim Ocheje, public statements, development priorities and published information.

For Journalists & Media

Media Enquiries & Requests

Journalists and media organisations may use this page to communicate requests for interviews, statements, background information, photographs or clarification of publicly available information.

Requests should clearly identify the media organisation, subject of the enquiry and any relevant deadline.

Before Submitting an Enquiry

Please provide accurate details and sufficient context. Requests concerning official electoral information, legal matters or government announcements should also be independently verified through the appropriate authorities.
