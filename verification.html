Information Integrity

Verify Before You Share

A simple guide to checking public information, identifying reliable sources and avoiding misinformation or impersonation.

Verification Guide

Information Should Be Checked Carefully

Public information can change quickly. Before sharing a claim, announcement, quotation, event information or electoral information, visitors are encouraged to check the source and confirm that the information is current.

01

Check the Source

Identify where the information came from and whether the source is an official institution, recognized publication or clearly identified public-information channel.

02

Check the Date

An old report can sometimes be presented as a new development. Always check when information was originally published or updated.

03

Check the Context

Headlines, screenshots and short quotations may not provide the complete context. Read the full material whenever possible.

04

Compare Sources

Important claims should, where possible, be compared against independent and reliable sources before being treated as confirmed.

05

Watch for Impersonation

Be cautious of accounts, messages or websites that claim to represent a person or organization without clear confirmation.

06

Verify Official Matters

Election dates, procedures, registration information and other official matters should be confirmed through the appropriate government or electoral authority.

Before Sharing

Five Questions to Ask

Taking a few moments to check information can help reduce the spread of inaccurate or misleading claims.

1

Who published it?

Identify the person, organization or publication behind the information.

2

When was it published?

Check whether the information is recent or relates to an earlier event.

3

Is there supporting evidence?

Look for documents, official announcements or credible reporting supporting the claim.

4

Does another reliable source confirm it?

Compare significant claims with independent reliable sources where practical.

5

Am I certain before sharing?

If important information cannot be confirmed, consider waiting until reliable evidence becomes available.

Important Notice

The appearance of information on this website does not by itself make every statement an official government announcement.

Information concerning elections, laws, government procedures or other official matters should be verified with the appropriate official authority.

Visitors should also be cautious about screenshots, forwarded messages, anonymous social-media posts and unofficial websites claiming to represent individuals or organizations.

If you encounter a questionable statement, suspected impersonation or information that appears misleading, you can contact the website through the available contact page.
