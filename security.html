Security Information

Protecting a Safe Online Experience

We aim to maintain a responsible and secure online environment for visitors accessing information about John Ibrahim Ocheje, public activities, policy interests, news, resources and engagement opportunities.

🔒

Responsible Website Use

Visitors are expected to use this website lawfully and responsibly and should not attempt to interfere with its operation or security.

🛡

Information Protection

Information submitted through available website channels should be treated responsibly and handled in accordance with the applicable privacy information published on this website.

✓

Safe Browsing

Visitors should exercise care when opening external websites, downloading files or providing information through third-party services.

!

Suspicious Activity

Suspected impersonation, fraudulent activity or attempts to misuse this website should be treated seriously and reported through the available contact channel.

Security Practices

Principles for a Safer Website

Good website security involves both responsible website administration and responsible visitor behaviour.

01

Protect Your Personal Information

Avoid submitting passwords, banking information, private identification numbers or other sensitive information through an inappropriate website channel.

02

Verify Official Information

Information concerning elections, government procedures, laws and official announcements should be confirmed through the appropriate official authority.

03

Beware of Impersonation

Be cautious of individuals or accounts claiming to represent this website or its associated activities without appropriate confirmation.

04

Use Secure Devices

Visitors should keep their phones, computers, browsers and operating systems reasonably updated and protected.

05

Do Not Attempt Unauthorized Access

Attempts to gain unauthorized access to website systems, interfere with website operations or bypass security measures are not permitted.

Online Safety

Stay Alert Online

Political and public-interest websites can sometimes be imitated by fraudulent pages or accounts. Visitors should carefully verify information before acting on requests for money, personal information or account credentials.

  • Verify the website address before submitting information.
  • Be cautious of unexpected payment requests.
  • Do not share passwords or security codes.
  • Verify suspicious claims through reliable sources.
  • Report suspected impersonation or fraudulent activity.

Your Security Matters

This website will not ask visitors to disclose passwords, banking PINs, security codes or other confidential credentials through ordinary public communication channels.

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to represent this website, verify the information independently before taking action.

Important Security Notice

No online environment can be guaranteed to be completely free from security risks. Visitors should take reasonable precautions when browsing websites, following external links or submitting information online.

This security page provides general information and does not replace professional cybersecurity advice or applicable law.

Have a Security Concern?

If you notice suspicious activity, misleading information or a possible impersonation connected with this website, please use the contact page to provide relevant information.
