ABOUT THE ASPIRANT

Leadership With Purpose

Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje is seeking the mandate of the people of Idah Federal Constituency to serve in the House of Representatives in 2027.

His vision is built around transparent representation, practical development, responsible leadership and meaningful engagement with the people.

The campaign believes that effective representation must listen to the people, understand their challenges and pursue solutions that create lasting opportunities.

Transparent Representation Accountability and openness in public service.
People-Centred Leadership Putting citizens and communities at the heart of representation.
Development Focus Supporting practical solutions and sustainable growth.
THE AGENDA

Six Points for Better Representation

A focused agenda built around accountability, economic opportunity, quality representation, infrastructure, social programmes and youth development.

01

Transparency & Accountability

Provide and ensure transparency, robust engagements and accountability on constituency projects.

02

Gas & Oil Opportunities

Leverage experience in oil and gas to develop structures for the equitable utilization of Kogi oil resources.

03

Quality Representation

Provide quality representation through thorough and knowledge-based engagements.

04

Infrastructure Partnerships

Interface with appropriate NGOs and partners to bridge infrastructure gaps across communities.

05

Expand Current Windows Programme

Support the expansion and improvement of existing opportunities and programmes that benefit constituents.

06

Youth Development

Build robust youth development programmes for young people and the less privileged.
