2027 HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES ASPIRATION

Six-Point Agenda

An overview of the six areas identified in the published agenda associated with Engr. John Ibrahim Ocheje and Idah Federal Constituency.

Published Agenda

Six Areas of Focus

The following points are presented as the key areas of focus displayed in the campaign material.

01

Transparency & Accountability

A focus on transparent engagement, accountability and responsible handling of constituency projects and public representation.

02

Gas & Oil Opportunities

Exploring opportunities connected with gas and oil resources and leveraging relevant experience and partnerships.

03

Quality Representation

Emphasis on thorough engagement and knowledge-based representation of constituency interests and concerns.

04

Infrastructure Partnerships

Working with appropriate organisations and partners to identify opportunities for addressing infrastructure needs.

05

Expand Current Windows Programme

Expansion of the existing windows programme as part of the stated development priorities.

06

Youth Development

Youth-focused development initiatives, including attention to opportunities for less-privileged communities.
