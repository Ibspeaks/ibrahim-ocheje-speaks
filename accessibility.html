Inclusive Digital Access

Accessibility

Our goal is to make this public information platform clear, accessible and usable for as many people as reasonably possible.

Accessibility Statement

Information Should Be Easy to Access

This website is designed to provide public information about John Ibrahim Ocheje, development priorities, public updates, resources and opportunities for engagement.

We aim to make the website usable across different devices, including mobile phones, tablets and computers.

Our Commitment

We will continue improving the clarity, navigation and usability of the website as the platform develops.

Website Features

Designed With Usability in Mind

Several design choices are intended to make the website easier to navigate and understand.

A

Clear Typography

The website uses readable fonts, clear headings and appropriate spacing to make information easier to follow.

↔

Responsive Design

Pages are designed to adapt to different screen sizes so that visitors can use the website on phones, tablets and computers.

☰

Simple Navigation

Main sections are organised through a consistent navigation structure to help visitors find information more easily.

✓

Structured Information

Content is divided into sections, cards and headings to make longer pages easier to scan.

Accessibility Principles

Four Areas We Focus On

Good accessibility involves more than visual design.

👁️

Readability

Clear text, headings and spacing help visitors read information.

📱

Device Access

Pages are designed for both mobile and larger screens.

🧭

Navigation

Consistent menus and links help visitors move around the site.

💬

Communication

Important information is presented in straightforward language.

Using the Website

If You Experience a Difficulty

If part of the website is difficult to use, unclear or does not work properly on your device, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

  • Tell us which page or section caused the difficulty.
  • Describe the problem as clearly as possible.
  • Where appropriate, tell us what device or browser you are using.
  • We will consider accessibility feedback when improving the site.

Accessibility Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing process. Some content, documents, third-party services or external websites may not be fully accessible or may have accessibility features outside our control. We will seek to improve the experience as new content and features are added.

Need Help Finding Information?

Explore our sitemap, resources and FAQ, or contact us if you need assistance locating information on the website.
